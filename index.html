Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need a medical exam?
For most plans we offer, no. Simplified-issue and final expense plans use a few health questions instead of an exam.
How much coverage can I get?
Final expense plans run $1,000–$30,000, and other life products go higher — up to $500,000 with Occidental's Express UL. Your agent will confirm what fits you.
Will my premium go up?
On whole life and final expense plans, no — once issued, premiums never increase and benefits never decrease.
Can I talk to a person first?
Always. Call (870) 243-4114 and a licensed agent will go over your options with you. You can also reach our assistant any time at (888) 616-4602.
